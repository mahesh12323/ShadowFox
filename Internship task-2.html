Season Recap

The 2026 Run

The IPL 2026 season wrapped on May 31. Here's how RCB defended the crown.

League Stage

Nine wins from fourteen, top of the table

RCB topped the league stage and went straight into Qualifier 1 — the only route that offers two shots at the final.

Qualifier 1

Past Gujarat Titans, into the final

A win over GT in the qualifier booked RCB's spot in the final without needing the Eliminator route.

The Final — May 31, Ahmedabad

RCB 161/5 beat Gujarat Titans 155/8, by 5 wickets

Bowlers Rasikh Salam, Josh Hazlewood and Bhuvneshwar Kumar held GT to 155/8. Virat Kohli's unbeaten 75 off 42 sealed back-to-back titles.

History

Only the third team to go back-to-back

RCB joined Chennai Super Kings (2010–11) and Mumbai Indians (2019–20) as the only franchises to successfully defend an IPL title.
